06 / Philosophy

Physical records and propositions

Photographed material, short philosophical propositions, and evidence of how an invariant is altered by embodiment and documentation.

Archive opening · records to be added

Format

Each entry may contain one proposition, one physical photograph or scan, a date and material description, plus a concise field note. The images are evidence, not decoration: material surfaces, marks, and objects carry a different form of persistence than a digital image.

Proposition zero

A structure does not persist by refusing change. It persists by carrying enough relation through change to remain legible after translation.
